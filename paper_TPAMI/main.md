# Task- and Budget-Conditioned Representation Selection for Heterogeneous Time Series

## Abstract

Heterogeneous time series differ in sampling, observation support and target structure, but a common embedding interface does not determine which representation a finite predictor can exploit. We study representation utility jointly with the target, consumer family and resource budget. A statistically supervised message provides one controlled reparameterization of a complete ordinary code; its deterministic dependence separates possible information loss from finite-model fitting effects. We then distinguish selection of a single fixed predictor, static predictive fusion and acquisition-conditioned policies. A paired held-out comparison treats these as complete policies and accounts for multiplicity, declared cost and explicit distribution-shift assumptions. Finite counterexamples show that zero hard-selection headroom does not rule out fusion and that a source-supported policy can fail after target conditional order reverses. These results motivate a multi-domain protocol that tests representation and policy choices without treating extra supervision, unpriced computation or target-based selection as evidence of a better representation.

## 1. Introduction

Representations are useful relative to a prediction target and the models that consume them, not solely because they share a tensor shape. Classical representation learning emphasizes this relationship [@bengio2013representation], and modern time-series models face it across varying dimensionality, temporal resolution and observation support. MOMENT and UniTS demonstrate broad multi-task and multi-dataset modeling [@goswami2024moment; @gao2024units]. A general representation claim must therefore explain which information is preserved, which computations become easier and under which evaluation budget the change is useful.

Temporal heterogeneity has prompted several effective modeling strategies. Neural CDE treats observations through continuous controls, while t-PatchGNN, Hi-Patch and HyperIMTS model irregular or asynchronous structure with different patch and graph constructions [@kidger2020cde; @zhang2024tpatchgnn; @luo2025hipatch; @li2025hyperimts]. Moirai-MoE replaces frequency-based specialization with token-level sparse experts [@liu2025moiraimoe]. The recent AME-TS preprint uses structural descriptors to supervise expert specialization [@wang2026amets]. These precedents rule out a broad claim of first adaptive heterogeneous time-series routing. They instead motivate testing whether a proposed representation is preferable to strong single and static-fusion policies when its information access and cost are explicitly matched.

Probabilistic targets make the comparison more demanding. Neural Laplace, CSDI and LLapDiff provide continuous-time or conditional generative mechanisms [@holt2022neurallaplace; @tashiro2021csdi; @you2026llapdiff]. We inherit these capabilities rather than rename them. A physical modal coefficient, an observed forecast target and a pretrained reference latent are different random variables. Improving a posterior approximation for one does not guarantee that a finite consumer improves on another. Gaussian and finite-mixture heads remain necessary controls for any asserted value of an iterative diffusion model.

The analytical foundations are already substantial. Score compression and Bayesian goal-oriented approximation address retained information and target quantities [@alsing2018compression; @spantini2015lowrank; @spantini2017goal]. Approximate sufficiency connects compressed representations with conditional generation [@oko2025sufficiency]. Predictive V-information formalizes usable information under restricted observer families [@xu2020usable]. Consequently, a deterministic reparameterization cannot be described as creating new Bayes information, even when it helps a particular trained network. Our first controlled comparison uses an ordinary source-supervised code and a same-budget statistical message derived from that complete code, then varies the target and consumer rather than assuming a universal improvement.

Representation comparison also creates a selection problem. Multi-expert deferral distinguishes fixed predictors from joint optimization and includes costs in the decision objective [@mao2024regression; @mao2025routing]. A hard selector's conditional risk gap is not a theorem about a newly trained soft fusion model, and a favorable empirical minimum can arise from selecting on noisy validation estimates. Learn then Test provides a general risk-control framework for such calibration decisions [@angelopoulos2025ltt]. We specialize a paired bounded-loss comparison to a frozen family containing single predictors, static predictive mixtures and acquisition-based policies, and explicitly separate source-law evidence from assumptions needed to transfer a certificate.

The proposed study therefore has two linked but distinct targets. First, it measures how the utility of a statistically supervised message changes with target, consumer capacity and message budget. Second, it evaluates whether a complete policy improves over a strong source-selected reference when selection uses independent original groups and pays its declared cost. The architecture remains minimal: use the existing encoder and readout, add neither a new expert family nor a router unless an earlier fixed-policy comparison supports it. Moment supervision is checked at its actual global readout; ordinary features remain available when moments do not determine the full distribution. Finite heteroscedastic optimization is evaluated directly rather than inferred from the population score optimum [@gneiting2007proper; @seitzer2022pitfalls].

The empirical design spans clinical measurements, wearable activity, climate, energy and speech-feature sequences. Tasks and losses are kept separate, with more than one encoder and consumer family required for a general conclusion. The industrial HSE application is treated as related work from the companion TII study, not as a second independent discovery or a substitute for the external experiments. The current finite results establish selection and shift counterexamples; multi-domain learned comparisons are required before claiming a general method advantage.
