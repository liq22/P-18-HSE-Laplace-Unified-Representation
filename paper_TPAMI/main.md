# Task- and Budget-Conditioned Representation Selection for Heterogeneous Time Series

## Abstract

A common time-series embedding shape does not establish which coordinates a finite predictor can use effectively. We study conditional-moment reparameterization under explicit supervision, consumer and computation constraints. An ordinary code and its derived statistical message share one source-trained checkpoint. Analysis of the implemented head shows that its mean is affine, while the complete message can be invertible under a stated rank condition. We therefore introduce a same-head affine control that retains the learned raw head and ordinary tail before nonlinear covariance coordinates. The statistical and affine-head messages carry equivalent information on the ideal attainable image, making their difference a question of finite-model use rather than new observations. A coordinate-transported ridge objective supplies complementary controls for PCA, orthogonal and whitening alternatives. An official variable-length speech-feature experiment reproduces identical test classification decisions under these affine references; whitening changes the numerical conditioning without improving this outcome. A replay verifies a strictly common winning class across all seven models on every retained validation, calibration and test example, ruling out classification-label gains from hard selection or convex fusion of these fixed scores on those examples. These results define the alternatives that learned conditional-moment coordinates must exceed in task-specific industrial and multi-domain evaluations.

## 1. Introduction

Representation quality depends on the target and the model that consumes its coordinates, not simply a common tensor interface. Representation learning has long emphasized this dependence [@bengio2013representation]. MOMENT and UniTS enable broad time-series comparisons [@goswami2024moment; @gao2024units], but dataset coverage alone cannot establish why a particular structured message is preferable. The specific question is which predictive benefit remains after ordinary supervised codes and simple same-dimensional transformations receive comparable information and computation.

Irregular sampling and expert specialization already have strong precedents. Neural CDE, t-PatchGNN, Hi-Patch and HyperIMTS preserve temporal structure through continuous, patch and graph models [@kidger2020cde; @zhang2024tpatchgnn; @luo2025hipatch; @li2025hyperimts]. Moirai-MoE uses sparse experts, and AME-TS supervises expert organization through structural descriptors [@liu2025moiraimoe; @wang2026amets]. Neither heterogeneous tokenization nor structural routing by itself defines an unoccupied problem. Representation experiments must specify actual observation access, target variables and strong single/fusion alternatives.

Probabilistic prediction adds a target-specific requirement. Neural Laplace, CSDI and LLapDiff supply continuous-time and conditional-generation mechanisms [@holt2022neurallaplace; @tashiro2021csdi; @you2026llapdiff]. The reference latent, physical coefficients and observed labels are different targets. LLapDiff is retained as one finite consumer, not assumed necessary for every task. Classification starts with direct label heads; forecasting and imputation retain Gaussian/mixture controls. A lower native denoising loss does not substitute for improved diagnostic macro-F1 or an entire conditional posterior.

General statistical foundations are already available. Compression and goal-oriented Bayesian approximation address retained information and target quantities [@alsing2018compression; @spantini2015lowrank; @spantini2017goal]. Approximate sufficiency and predictive V-information relate information to generation and restricted computation [@oko2025sufficiency; @xu2020usable]. Our construction uses a complete supervised code R and its deterministic message M=T(R). Deterministic does not mean necessarily lossy: the implemented complete message can admit an inverse, and its affine mean alone cannot enlarge an affine prediction family. Generic projection identities are therefore interpretation tools rather than evidence of statistical specificity.

We isolate the nonlinear map within the actual frozen head. The control H_A sends the raw affine head output and the same ordinary tail, using the same supervision, weights, dimensionality and precision as M. The latter changes only covariance-coordinate parameterization. On the ideal attainable image, the two messages determine one another even if the head is not invertible as a map from R. This makes a comparison with H_A more specific than a comparison with untreated R. Finite precision, conditioning, consumer capacity and actual factorization cost still require measurement; equality of Bayes information neither predicts equal optimization nor validates moment estimates.

Simple coordinate controls remain essential. Full-dimensional PCA and orthogonal transformations preserve an isotropic solved-ridge objective. Whitening requires a coordinate-transported penalty to represent that same objective; leaving the penalty isotropic changes the learning problem. Source-only fitting, a fixed validation search and restored prediction checkpoints expose this distinction on real data. Gaussian scoring identifies moments only under its assumptions, and finite optimization needs separate diagnostics [@gneiting2007proper; @seitzer2022pitfalls]. Best single and static predictive fusion precede optional routing; established multi-expert/calibration results are secondary tools, not new generic contributions [@mao2024regression; @mao2025routing; @angelopoulos2025ltt].

The completed external reference uses Japanese Vowels, native-length LPC sequences and an explicitly simple time-mean representation. Its affine references produce the same held-out class decisions, a retained null result rather than a statistical-message advantage. The general evaluation is designed to vary target, encoder, consumer and budget across five task-compatible domains, with genuine learned features required before general conclusions. Industrial method evidence remains in the companion TII study and is not counted twice.

## 2. Contribution under evaluation

The candidate is a method-specific account of when conditional-moment coordinates improve a finite time-series consumer beyond an equally supervised ordinary code, its same trained affine head and generic transformations. The exact factorization, invertibility and penalty identities are classical supporting analysis. A distinct TPAMI contribution still requires genuine target/consumer/budget-dependent findings across multiple encoder/model families; neither affine reference accuracy nor a synthetic native loop supplies that evidence.
