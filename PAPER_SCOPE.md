## Shared without duplication

`src/hse_laplace/`, the existing numbered `theory/` corpus, `experiments/learned_conditioning/`, `experiments/p19/` and `literature/references.bib` are shared research code/antecedents. Do not copy a second trainer, data reader, posterior library or bibliography. The historical `p19` path is an experiment package, not a manuscript identifier.

The general fixed-policy argument and routing-control source CSV belong to `paper_TPAMI/`. The TII folder defines its source-supported industrial posterior formulation and retains industrial results. Its older analytical posterior and coordinate studies remain shared derivation/regression evidence and component controls, not proof that the new source-restricted native method has been trained.

PHM preparation, labels, raw group keys and accepted splits remain exclusively owned by PHMFactory. General external raw sources are documented only in `paper_TPAMI/experiments/DATA_DOWNLOAD_SOP.md`. Downloading an archive is not completing its conversion or model experiment.

## Publication boundary

Code and stated background results may be shared with attribution. TII owns the proposed industrial support-qualified inference mechanism, its coordinate/conditioner ablations and newly obtained industrial method evidence. TPAMI must establish additional target/consumer/budget-dependent conclusions across more than one encoder and consumer family, with independent policy selection and multi-domain evidence. The finite-policy certificate is a classical statistical specialization, not sufficient novelty by itself.

Before either submission, reconcile overlap, disclose the related manuscript/status and identify exactly which claims, tables and figures are reused as prior/shared material. Do not present the same result as independently new in both papers. If the general study yields only extra datasets and the same mechanism, retain it as an extension study rather than assert a second publication contribution.

## Working branches and completion

Both workspaces are maintained on `dev`; no second long-lived paper branch is needed. Do not modify `master`, force-push or delete others' branches. Scope separation is not scientific promotion: learned-model claims remain unsupported until the declared comparisons run. A negative or equivalent result can complete a task and lead to a simpler model.
