# Literature matrix — source-supported industrial posterior revision

Author reading record, 2026-09-18. The 31 original works below were read in the listed method/theory and corresponding experimental or analytical-validation sections. This is not a claim of auditing every appendix. Preprints are labelled; journal HSE and TF-ProFM are retained as additional direct citations but not counted here because their full journal texts were not obtained. Review articles are not used to meet the original-paper count. All 31 keys occur in the current Introduction or Related Work.

The source links identify the versions actually consulted. A conference citation does not imply that an inaccessible final PDF was inspected. “Unresolved here” identifies the question this study must test, not a claim that an earlier paper never considered it.

## Most decisive comparison

FISHER/HSE address industrial heterogeneous representations. CSDI/SSSD/DDNM already restrict generation using missingness or measurement structure. Ambient Diffusion and A-DPS address learning/using priors from corrupted observations. iVAE and the disentanglement counterexample prevent arbitrary latent-to-mechanical identification. None of these positive capabilities makes an arbitrary source union sufficient to identify the industrial conditional proposed here. The new study must establish its specific reference correspondence, conditional target and projected LLapDiff implementation, then test posterior-to-diagnosis transfer.

## Source-derived versus newly proposed

The capability descriptions and evidence columns are source-derived. The source-relative target definition, particular HSE–LLapDiff restriction and E1–E5 experimental pairing are proposed in this revision. The latter remain hypotheses until native implementation and industrial evidence exist. The generic algebra/KL/denoising identities are not reclaimed as original results.
