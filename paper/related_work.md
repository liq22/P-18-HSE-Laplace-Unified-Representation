# Related Work and checked novelty boundary

## Industrial and time-series context

HSE supplies the heterogeneous fault-diagnosis interface [@li2025hse]. The inspected FISHER arXiv v2 describes industrial multimodal representation and sampling-rate-related STFT sub-bands [@fan2026fisher]; this technical version is distinguished from the final TII article. It rules out a broad first-rate-aware-industrial-representation claim, without assuming anything about uninspected final-paper sections. TF-ProFM remains an industrial comparison candidate, not a claimed reproduced method.

Neural CDE, t-PatchGNN, ContiFormer, Hi-Patch and HyperIMTS represent irregular observations in different temporal/graph structures [@kidger2020cde; @zhang2024tpatchgnn; @chen2023contiformer; @luo2025hipatch; @li2025hyperimts]. MOMENT and UniTS provide broad multi-dataset/task references [@goswami2024moment; @gao2024units]. Compare each only on compatible tasks and actual official inputs; no fabricated likelihood is attached to a point forecaster.

## Generation and statistical conditioning

The full LLapDiff v2 method and Appendices E–H specify a reference latent target, history summarizer, modal denoiser and iterative reverse process [@you2026llapdiff]. Solver-free physical-time evaluation does not mean one-step diffusion inference. This study inherits the generator and changes the condition only.

Alsing–Wandelt, Oko and Spantini establish relevant compression/sufficiency/posterior-approximation foundations [@alsing2018compression; @oko2025sufficiency; @spantini2015lowrank; @spantini2017goal]. The earlier project notes identify Oko's Definition 1 and Proposition 3 as direct antecedents; the full text could not be newly fetched in this update, so no new negative scope claim is drawn from its abstract. Gaussian conditional scoring and heteroscedastic optimization are supported by Gneiting–Raftery and Seitzer et al. [@gneiting2007proper; @seitzer2022pitfalls]. Neither positive definiteness nor an analytic optimum proves finite neural calibration.

## Direct finite-model and routing predecessors

**Xu et al., ICLR 2020.** The inspected full paper (Definitions 1–3 and Proposition 1) defines predictive V-information through restricted predictive families. It explicitly allows computation to increase usable information while Shannon information obeys its own data-processing constraint. Consequently, our phrase “conditional accessibility” is an operational fixed-system risk profile, not a new general notion of usable information.

**Mao, Mohri and Zhong, ICML 2024/2025.** The official PMLR records and 2025 full-paper Section 2 distinguish jointly learned and fixed-expert two-stage deferral, with cost-sensitive losses and stronger learning analyses. Generic source-conditioned routing and a standard 2-epsilon plug-in argument are not our invention. Our application is the nested HSE pair, the acquisition-only selection restriction and its measured industrial utility. We do not import their H-consistency results for our unproved architecture or unbounded loss.
