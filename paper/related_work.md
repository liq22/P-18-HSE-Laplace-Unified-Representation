# Industrial closest work and contribution boundary

HSE, FISHER and TF-ProFM are direct industrial competitors [@li2025hse; @fan2026fisher; @pi2026tfprofm]. The inspected FISHER technical-report SectionsII-A/II-B use fixed-duration STFT windows/hops, sub-band processing and concatenated embeddings; SectionIII-B discusses source-recording separation (https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16696v2). Therefore arbitrary sampling-rate support, sub-band structure and recording-aware evaluation cannot be claimed as first contributions here. The corresponding final journal version was not fully inspected in this update; its uninspected sections are not treated as absent.

FISHER must be a closest baseline where a compatible official implementation and accepted PHM data protocol exist, with pretraining access, band-dependent output size and compute reported. A low-scale HSE study cannot claim superiority by excluding the sub-band comparator or comparing incompatible input bands. MFPT's small reference baseline is data-path acceptance, not that comparison.

Neural Laplace, CSDI and LLapDiff supply inherited generation mechanisms [@holt2022neurallaplace; @tashiro2021csdi; @you2026llapdiff]. The full LLapDiff v2 Section5.1 already consumes value/dynamics/time-gap/mask information; Sections5.2–5.3 define native modal prediction and reverse generation. Our industrial primary endpoint is diagnosis, and ordinary direct classifiers test whether generation is necessary at all.

Alsing/Spantini/Oko/Xu are direct statistical compression, target-aware approximation and finite-consumer precedents [@alsing2018compression; @spantini2015lowrank; @spantini2017goal; @oko2025sufficiency; @xu2020usable]. The actual same-head affine comparison narrows the method hypothesis to the nonlinear statistical coordinate map. Exact invertibility does not imply finite numerical stability or statistical calibration; it is not a new general information principle.

Proper scoring and finite training are distinct [@gneiting2007proper; @seitzer2022pitfalls]. Seitzer's inspected Section3 and Appendix D.5 show why heteroscedastic training and detached beta-NLL weighting require explicit treatment. The same checkpoint is retained across R, raw head and M, rather than changing supervision for the favored representation.

General multi-domain experiments and policy-selection analysis stay in the TPAMI companion. A genuine industrial method claim must exceed equally supervised ordinary codes, simple reparameterizations, direct classifiers and strong static fusion on original-recording-held-out data at declared cost. Neither missing abstracts nor inaccessible full texts justify novelty claims.
