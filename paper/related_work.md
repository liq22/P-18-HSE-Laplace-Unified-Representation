# Related Work

## 2.1 Heterogeneous representation and transferable diagnostic evidence

HSE addresses heterogeneous industrial input interfaces, while FISHER uses sampling-rate-dependent time–frequency sub-bands and industrial pretraining [@li2025hse; @fan2026fisher]. TF-ProFM is a further industrial time–frequency/prototype reference [@pi2026tfprofm]. These methods define strong comparison points for structured inputs; neither variable-rate support nor a physical feature block is sufficient novelty by itself. TS-TCC learns through temporally augmented cross-view prediction and contextual contrast, whereas TF-C uses separate temporal and spectral encoders with a consistency objective [@eldele2021tstcc; @zhang2022tfc]. TF-C's reported cross-dataset transfer includes target fine-tuning, which must be distinguished from source-only evaluation on an untouched dataset.

Domain Separation Networks distinguish shared and private features using reconstruction, difference and adaptation objectives [@bousmalis2016dsn]. Their private component is statistically domain-specific, not automatically the null-space complement of a known sensor operator. Invariant domain-adaptation theory also exposes the effect of label/conditional mismatch, and DomainBed demonstrates the importance of an explicit model-selection procedure [@zhao2019invariant; @gulrajani2021domainbed]. Together these results establish why private information, label compatibility and source-only selection matter. They do not make different industrial datasets paired realizations of the same event. Our proposed role partition is defined by a declared acquisition experiment in aligned coordinates; its cross-dataset validity is an assumption to verify, not an outcome of marginal alignment alone.

## 2.2 Learning latent structure from partial views

MVAE combines available modality experts and trains across observed modality subsets; MMVAE uses a mixture-based variational posterior and evaluates joint/cross-modal generation [@wu2018mvae; @shi2019mmvae]. Both provide direct missing-view inference precedents. Their latent laws rely on the joint training design and specified modeling assumptions; the availability of each modality somewhere does not alone identify every cross-modal conditional. Identifiable-VAE theory makes the role of auxiliary variation and structural assumptions explicit, while the impossibility result for unsupervised disentanglement rules out a general interpretation of arbitrary latent coordinates as recovered factors [@khemakhem2020ivae; @locatello2019disentanglement].

For industrial use, a shared fault label is not an alignment map between modal coefficients. Sensor position can mix modes, and machine dynamics can change the map from a state to a waveform. We require either a source-calibrated physical dictionary or a validated block-structured reference latent before imposing a physical support mask. Without that correspondence, the target is a conditional distribution over learned features, not identified mechanical modes. This restriction concerns the scientific meaning of the proposed generator, rather than a limitation inferred from an earlier paper's unmentioned implementation detail.

## 2.3 Conditional diffusion, measurement constraints and corrupted-data priors

DDPM supplies Gaussian forward perturbations and a learned reverse process; latent diffusion separates compression from a generative model in latent space [@ho2020ddpm; @rombach2022latent]. DDRM uses the measurement operator's singular directions to combine a diffusion prior and linear noisy observations [@kawar2022ddrm]. DDNM explicitly separates range-space consistency and null-space generation [@wang2023ddnm]. DPS approximates likelihood guidance for noisy linear and nonlinear inverse problems [@chung2023dps]. These methods already address measurement-conditioned generation, so neither an observation mask nor a null-space projection is an independent first contribution here.

The decisive distinction is the source model. Ambient Diffusion learns from corrupted observations under conditions on the corruption ensemble, and Consistent Diffusion Meets Tweedie studies distribution learning with noisy-only data and consistency constraints [@daras2023ambient; @daras2024consistent]. A-DPS transfers such a prior into inverse reconstruction with a potentially different corruption/measurement operator [@aali2025ambientdps]. These are the closest prior formulations of learning a prior without complete clean sources. Our narrower industrial question conditions the *permitted target* on source-observable support and conditional identifiability, while examining a Laplace-modal temporal parameterization and diagnostic transfer. This is a proposed joint mechanism; novelty is not established by the projection or the phrase “source-supported” in isolation.

## 2.4 Temporal dynamics and missing-only time-series generation

Neural CDE encodes irregular observations through a controlled continuous-time path [@kidger2020cde]. Neural Laplace models trajectories through an inverse-Laplace representation, including systems beyond ordinary differential equations [@holt2022neurallaplace]. TimeGrad uses autoregressive diffusion for probabilistic multivariate forecasting; CSDI learns a conditional score on designated missing entries; SSSD combines conditional diffusion with structured state-space models and compares diffusion over all versus missing-only components [@rasul2021timegrad; @tashiro2021csdi; @alcaraz2023sssd]. Their capabilities make ordinary time-aware encoders, missing-only conditional diffusion and non-Laplace temporal denoisers necessary controls.

LLapDiff predicts stable modal parameters conditioned on observed values, dynamics proxies, time gaps and masks, synthesizing a continuous-time reference-latent trajectory [@you2026llapdiff]. The modal parameters are predicted during denoising and are not automatically identifiable physical poles. Its Laplace dynamics and its stochastic diffusion operate on different mathematical objects; heavy-tailed Laplace-noise motivation is therefore inapplicable. The proposed extension needs a block-preserving link from industrial acquisition support to the latent trajectory updated by LLapDiff. If the reference decoder mixes supposedly supported and unsupported blocks, an output mask alone cannot establish physical support preservation.

## 2.5 Compression, uncertainty and downstream value

Score compression preserves Fisher information in its stated local setting, and prior-/goal-oriented low-rank approximation targets posterior quantities under known forward/noise models [@alsing2018compression; @spantini2015lowrank; @spantini2017goal]. These results are strong Gaussian-oracle baselines rather than evidence that a learned HSE token is sufficient. Approximate-sufficiency theory already connects conditional information loss and diffusion prediction, and predictive V-information explicitly depends on a restricted observer family [@oko2025sufficiency; @xu2020usable]. We use the corresponding KL and conditional-expectation identities as inherited analytical tools. The existing ordinary-code, same-head affine and conditional-moment messages test one part of the HSE–LLapDiff interface; they do not replace the source-supported posterior problem.

Proper scoring rules assess distributions rather than just point predictions [@gneiting2007proper]. Nevertheless, heteroscedastic neural training can fail to reach the population moment optimum, and uncertainty quality can deteriorate under distribution shift [@seitzer2022pitfalls; @ovadia2019uncertainty]. A calibrated marginal interval does not establish an accurate joint latent posterior or a better diagnostic decision. Accordingly, exact posterior KL is reserved for known-law experiments; sample-based proper scores, conditional coverage, diagnostic macro-F1 and unsupported-output measurements are reported separately on their respective valid targets. A simple observed-only classifier or a well-fitted finite-mixture posterior may invalidate the need for the more complex generator.

## 2.6 Precise unresolved interaction

Prior work establishes heterogeneous industrial representations, missing-view inference, measurement-constrained diffusion and identifiable corrupted-data learning under specified conditions. The unresolved interaction tested here is whether **a source-identified inference target, an acquisition-aware HSE condition and a stable temporal diffusion parameterization together improve source-only cross-dataset industrial diagnosis**, beyond same-information simple posteriors and ordinary conditioners. The comparisons hold the latent target, source supervision and diagnostic protocol fixed. The proposal neither assumes that all missing variables are recoverable nor equates posterior generation with new observation information.
