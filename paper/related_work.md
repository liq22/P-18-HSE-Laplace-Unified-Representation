# Related Work — industrial manuscript

HSE, FISHER and TF-ProFM are direct industrial representation references [@li2025hse; @fan2026fisher; @pi2026tfprofm]. HSE already addresses heterogeneous fault signals; the inspected FISHER technical version describes industrial modalities and rate-dependent time–frequency structure. These precedents exclude a generic first-industrial-unification claim. Final-paper details not inspected are not inferred from missing abstracts.

Neural Laplace, CSDI and LLapDiff motivate the inherited continuous-time/probabilistic model [@holt2022neurallaplace; @tashiro2021csdi; @you2026llapdiff]. The inspected LLapDiff v2 distinguishes reference latent targets, history conditioning, stable modal prediction and iterative reverse generation. Our actual comparison changes its condition rather than claiming the generator is new.

Alsing–Wandelt, Spantini, Oko and Xu supply compression, goal-oriented posterior and finite-observer antecedents [@alsing2018compression; @spantini2015lowrank; @spantini2017goal; @oko2025sufficiency; @xu2020usable]. Proper scoring and heteroscedastic optimization are grounded in Gneiting–Raftery and Seitzer [@gneiting2007proper; @seitzer2022pitfalls]. They define the boundary of the industrial contribution: a concrete supervised message tested under industrial acquisition changes, not newly invented general information theory.

Best single, static prediction fusion and acquisition selection are empirical industrial controls. The general multi-expert/risk-calibration literature and broad external benchmark comparison now belong to `../paper_TPAMI/related_work.md`. No industrial result is claimed from an unrun external dataset or an upstream method's own headline score.
